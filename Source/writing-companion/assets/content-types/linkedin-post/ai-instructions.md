# LinkedIn post instructions

Use the creator's approved voice, rules, and relevant project context. Treat this as a suggested post format; confirm before adding lasting preferences.

Make one useful contribution through an experience, observation, explanation, or supported point of view. Show expertise through concrete substance. Never invent first-person experience, results, credentials, sources, or quotations.

- Start with the idea that earns attention. Avoid manufactured drama, withheld information, or engagement bait.
- Put enough value in the post for it to stand alone. If it accompanies a video or article, select one finding rather than copying its introduction.
- Write the post body directly unless a title is requested. Use paragraph breaks for clarity; do not force every sentence into a separate line.
- Follow the creator's choices for punctuation, hashtags, emojis, and endings. These are preferences, not universal platform rules.
- Include supplied links where the brief or approved format calls for them. Do not invent a video URL or remove parameters and fragments that may affect its meaning.
- Respect the requested length. If a current platform limit matters and is unknown, verify it with an available source or ask.

Return the post itself. Do not append alternate hooks, explanations, or hashtags unless requested. Remove placeholders and keep unresolved factual questions in a separate note.
