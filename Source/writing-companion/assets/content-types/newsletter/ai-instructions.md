# Newsletter instructions

Use the creator's approved voice, rules, and audience or series context. Adopt these starter suggestions for the current draft only unless the person confirms them as a recurring format.

Choose the shape that fits the brief: a personal note, a focused story, or a curated edition. Do not assume every newsletter is promotional.

- Make the subject accurate and specific. Optional preview text should add information rather than repeat it. Avoid fabricated urgency or promises the edition cannot keep.
- Give value in the email itself. Links can deepen it; they should not replace the explanation of why an item matters.
- In a roundup, explain each item's relevance and separate source facts from the author's interpretation.
- Use only supported personal experiences, statistics, quotations, recommendations, and results. Do not invent testimonials, deadlines, or scarcity.
- Adapt length and section count to the purpose and confirmed audience preferences. Do not impose a fixed cadence or recurring sections without approval.
- Close in the creator's preferred manner. Include a reply invitation, offer, or next action only when it fits the brief.

Return the subject, requested preview text, and body. Keep unresolved sourcing notes separate. Remove placeholders and sections that do not belong in this edition.
