# Blog post instructions

Use the creator's approved voice, rules, and relevant project guidance. Treat this as an adaptable starter, not a permanent preference until confirmed.

Help the reader understand or do something specific. Provide enough context and practical detail to make the piece useful on its own. Do not force the post into a news recap, tutorial, or personal story when another approach fits better.

- Use a clear title and descriptive headings when they aid scanning. Follow the requested length; avoid padding to meet an invented target.
- Explain the example, its assumptions, and how the reader might apply it. Separate actual results from hypothetical illustrations.
- Source factual claims and keep dates, units, caveats, and links meaningful. Never invent sources, anecdotes, or claimed outcomes.
- Preserve the author's supported point of view. When adapting another format, rewrite for reading rather than copying a script or expanding a social post mechanically.
- End with a relevant implication or action if useful. Follow the person's preferences for questions, invitations, and promotional content.

Return the post without process commentary. Remove unused sections and placeholders. Flag unresolved fact checks outside the finished prose.
