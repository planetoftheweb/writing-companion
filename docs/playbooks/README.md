# Video playbooks

Two short guides for making video with AI, built from real work. They go well with Writing Companion: the writing playbook shows how to build the files the skill helps you set up, and links to its starter templates and prompts.

| Playbook | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [Writing for Video with AI](ai-video-writing-playbook.pdf) | Your soul, rules, POV and format files, how to keep them alive, prompts to copy, AI helpers for research and rewrites, a draft check, and tips for writing for the ear and the screen |
| [Editing Video with AI](ai-video-editing-playbook.pdf) | Recording like you're editing, AI cleanup in Descript, making the video shorter, prompts for an AI editor, finishing touches, visuals you can make in minutes, and turning one recording into many videos |

Each one is three pages, for anyone from a first video to a hundredth. The tools named are the ones I use. Most editors and AI assistants have similar features.
