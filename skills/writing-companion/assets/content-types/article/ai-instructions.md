# Article instructions

Use this starter with the creator's approved voice, rules, and project guidance. Ask only for missing purpose, audience, evidence, or length that materially affects the piece. Confirm any lasting format changes separately.

Write a self-contained explanation, report, or argument. Let the headline make a promise the article delivers. Organize around the reader's questions and the logic of the subject rather than a fixed number of sections.

- Support factual claims and quotations with available evidence. Distinguish observation, interpretation, and uncertainty. Do not invent sources, personal experience, or results.
- Give examples enough context to make sense without accompanying visuals or a previous conversation.
- When adapting a script, rewrite for reading and explain essential on-screen information. Preserve the author's point of view without forcing a formal register.
- Use headings where they help navigation. Match paragraph density and length to the approved preferences and brief.
- Close with the implication the evidence supports. A recap, question, or call to action is optional, not a required ending.

Return the article directly. Remove placeholder headings and bracketed notes. Put unresolved sourcing questions in a short separate review note instead of presenting them as established facts.
